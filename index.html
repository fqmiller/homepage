F. Q. Miller

Speculative fiction author and information science researcher. Blending the imaginative reach of creative fiction and non-fiction with the empirical patience of collaborative social science research. Exploring how people, machines, and the living world are learning to co-exist, co-evolve, and share a future.

Creative Works

Her first degree was in history, psychology, literature and creative writing, with a focus on short stories, novels, and screenplay writing. She writes speculative fiction in which technology and the natural world act as characters in the same story, each shaping and shaped by the humans caught between them.

Social Research in Information and Technology

How do people, and the more-than-human world around them, build shared understanding of complex systems — technological, ecological, and social — through everyday experience of information?

A Master's and PhD in information science ground her fiction in real questions and empirical findings from research communities. For over two decades she has conducted research and written fiction of a speculative nature, side by side, with each sharpening the other.
